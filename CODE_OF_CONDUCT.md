# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `agy-workflow` a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer at [@khoawatt](https://github.com/khoawatt).
